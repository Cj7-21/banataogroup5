Activity 4: Flash Fiction

Write a Flash Fiction

Instructions: In a document, write a flash fiction in about 5 to 10 sentences. The flash fiction should tell the whole story that you wanted to tell using less than 1000 words.

Writing Parameters

  • Sentence Limit: Exactly 5 to 10 sentences.
  • Word Count: Under 1,000 words.
  • Narrative Completeness: The story must tell a complete arc (beginning, climax, and resolution).
