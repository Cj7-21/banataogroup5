Traditional Literary Genres

Usually appears in printed form and follows conventional literary structures.

Conventional = Traditional

  • Characteristics: Mostly text-based, intended for print.
  • Examples: Poetry and Prose.

21st Century Literary Genres

Emerged because of advancement in technology, digital media, and changing reading habits.

  • Characteristics: Combination of words and images, audio, hyperlinks, or interactive elements.

Types of 21st Century Literary Genres

  • Flash Fiction: A complete story told in fewer than 1,000 words.
  • Graphic Novel: A narrative presented through sequential art and text.
  • Doodle Fiction: A story that combines handwritten text with illustrations, sketches, or doodles.
  • Text-talk Novel: A story told primarily through text messages or chats.
  • Hyper Poetry: Poetry that includes hyperlinks, images, sounds, or interactive features.
  • Digi-Fiction: Literature combining written text with multimedia such as videos, audio, or animation.

Flash Fiction Example

"She bought two tickets to Paris, packed a single suitcase, and waited at the gate until the final call. When his phone went straight to voicemail, she boarded alone and sat in the window seat. As the plane lifted into the clouds, she took a deep breath and smiled."
