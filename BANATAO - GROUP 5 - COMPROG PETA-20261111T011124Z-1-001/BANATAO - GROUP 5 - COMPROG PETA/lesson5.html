Literary

Relating to written works—especially those considered to have artistic value, creative merit, or lasting quality (such as novels, poetry, plays, and essays).

Context

The background, environment, or circumstances surrounding a specific event, statement, or idea.

Answers the questions: when, why, where, and what.

Literary Context

Works to provide the reader with information about certain events and experiences in a piece of literature that would otherwise not be obvious.

Dimensions of Context

  • Author's Context: Everything surrounding the conception/creation of the work. Experience of the Author and his life.
  • Text Context: The world inside the body of work including its genre, structure, and internal setting.
  • Reader's Context: The background, values, time period, and experiences of the readers in relation to the text.

Types of Context

  • Historical Context
  • Cultural Context
  • Social Context
  • Political Context
  • Religious Context
  • Biographical Context

Historical Context

Refers to a specific period of time, the major events that took place during it, and the prevailing attitudes or conditions (social, political, economic, religious) of people living in that era.

Examples: Mi Ultimo Adios, Pag-ibig sa Tinubuang Lupa

Cultural Context

Refers to the traditions, customs, beliefs, language, and values of people belonging to a specific culture or country.

Example: Koreans preparing seaweed soup during birthdays

Social Context

Focuses on societal events, social hierarchies, class structures, and how societal expectations influence character attitudes and treatment.

Examples: Stories about slavery, Social Inequality

Political Context

Involves the political climate, government rules, power struggles, and dominant political views during the era of the text.

Example: The Safe House

Religious Context

Concerns the rules, customs, values, and practices of specific religious faiths.

Example: Senakulo

Biographical Context

Refers to information about the author’s own life, background, and personal experiences.

Examples: Mi Ultimo Adios, Pag-ibig sa Tinubuang Lupa

Note

These contexts often overlap in literary works.
