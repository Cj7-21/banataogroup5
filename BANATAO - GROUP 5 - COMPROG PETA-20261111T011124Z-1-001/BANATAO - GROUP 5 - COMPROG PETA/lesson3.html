Literature vs literature

  • literature: "designates any written text"
  • Literature: "designates a much smaller set of texts"
  • The literature on any given subject without much difficulty.
  • A subset of all the text that have been written.
  • Simply put, all written things can be considered literature but not all of them are Literature.

Forms of Literature & Literary Types (Genres)

  • Forms and Types of Literature
  • Forms of Literature: 2 Primary Forms: Poetry and Prose
    • Poetry: metered lines, stanzas, and rhythmic structures
    • Prose: standard sentence and paragraphs that run margin to margin
  • Literary Genre: classification of literature based on the technique, tone, content, or length of the writing.

Types of Poetry

  • Lyric Poetry
  • Narrative Poetry
  • Dramatic Poetry

Types of Poetry: Lyric Poetry

Expresses the poet's personal emotions, feelings, and thoughts. Unlike stories, lyric poems usually focus on a single emotion or experience.

Subtypes:

  • Love Poems
  • Odes
  • Sonnets
  • Elegies
  • Haiku

Types of Poetry: Narrative Poetry

Tells a story using poetic language and has characters.

Subtypes:

  • Epic
  • Ballad
  • Metrical Tale

Types of Poetry: Dramatic Poetry

Written to be performed or spoken by one or more characters. It often presents conflict through dialogue or a dramatic monologue.

Examples/Performances:

  • Playwright Theater
  • Spoken Performance

Types of Prose

  • Fiction: prose created from the writer's imagination
  • Non-fiction: prose that is based on facts

Prose Subtypes

  • Fiction: Short Stories, Novels, Fairy Tale, Science Fiction, Historical Fiction
  • Non-fiction: Biography, Autobiography, Essays, Newspaper, Textbooks, Encyclopedia
