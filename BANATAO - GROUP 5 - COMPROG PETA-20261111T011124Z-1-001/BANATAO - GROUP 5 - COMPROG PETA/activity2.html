Activity 2: The Safe House

Instructions: Read carefully and answer the following questions on the space provided or in your activity notebook.

THE SAFE HOUSE

By: Sandra Nicole Roldan

"The house is not safe. Inside, there are many locksets and bolts on the doors, and the windows are grilled. But the people who come in and out do not use keys. They knock in code. Three short taps, a pause, then two quick thuds.

My father tells me they are my uncles and aunts, but they change their names every few months. Sometimes Uncle Johnny becomes Uncle Frankie. Sometimes Auntie Marta is suddenly Auntie Clara. They bring backpacks stuffed with thick, roughly mimeographed papers that smell like gasoline and old dirt. They sit around our kitchen table until dawn, speaking in whispers that sound like dry leaves scraping across the floor.

My mother does not whisper. She packs her bags in the light of day. She tells me she is tired of cooking giant pots of rice for people who do not exist on paper. When she leaves, she does not use the code. She just turns the knob, steps out into the sun, and closes the door firmly behind her.

The house feels bigger now, and much colder, even when the kitchen is full."
