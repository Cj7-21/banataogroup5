Comparative Reading

Paragraph 1: Lila planted a seed. After several weeks, a plant grew.

Paragraph 2: Every morning before sunrise, Lila carefully watered the only seed she had left. Although the ground was dry and cracked, she refused to give up. When the first green shoot finally appeared, she smiled with tears in her eyes.

Question: Which paragraph is more interesting?

Close Reading

What is Close Reading?

It is a careful and purposeful reading of a text to discover deeper meanings by paying attention to the author's language, structure, literary elements, and supporting details.

Principles of Close Reading

  • CAREFUL = NOT RUSHED
  • WE ALSO ASK MORE QUESTIONS

Questions to Ask During Close Reading

  • Why did the author choose this word?
  • What does this symbol represent?
  • How does the setting affect the story?
  • What message is the author communicating?
  • Which details support my interpretation?

Literary Elements

  • Character
  • Setting
  • Conflict
  • Plot
  • Theme

Understanding the Elements

  • Character: Who is the story about?
  • Setting: Where does the story happen?
  • Conflict: What is the main struggle?

Story Conflicts

  • Lila vs the drought
  • Lila vs her own doubts

Plot Structure

The plot consists of:

  • Beginning
  • Rising Action
  • Climax
  • Falling Action
  • Resolution

Author's Purpose & Theme

  • Author's Purpose: Why did the author write this?
  • Theme vs. Topic:
    • Topic ≠ Theme
    • Example Topic: Hope
    • Example Theme: Hope grows through perseverance
